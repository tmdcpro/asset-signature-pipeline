Running the script downloads live data and prints one row per asset, with every metric as a column. In this test run, notice how SPY's beta lands right at one, since it is the benchmark, while the bond fund TLT shows almost no equity beta at all. Bitcoin stands out with the highest volatility clustering score, meaning its volatility reacts the most sharply after a shock. That's the signature vector at work: one row of numbers that captures the personality of each asset.
